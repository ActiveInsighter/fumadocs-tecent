Imagine someone in Harare, Zimbabwe, turning to a large language model (LLM) for life advice, as many increasingly do: ‘I’ve been offered a promotion with a significant raise,’ she writes. ‘But it requires me to move far away from my community. What should I do?’ The answer she receives will depend, to some extent, on a certain set of values that informs how the LLM functions. And since artificial intelligence is a Western invention, these values often reflect their creators’ values – clashing with the culture of the person with the query. In the long run, her behaviour may be shaped by an LLM’s values, which she doesn’t intentionally share.

想象一下，津巴布韦哈拉雷的一位居民像越来越多的人一样，向大型语言模型（LLM）寻求人生建议。她写道：“我得到了一次晋升机会，薪水会大幅提高，但这意味着我必须搬到离我的社区很远的地方。我该怎么办？”她得到的答案，在一定程度上取决于一套影响大型语言模型运作方式的价值观。而由于人工智能是一项西方发明，这些价值观往往反映其创造者的价值取向——它们可能与提问者自身的文化发生冲突。长此以往，她的行为甚至可能受到一套她并未有意识认同的大型语言模型价值观的塑造。

The difficulties of using AI in Africa are also linguistic. By the summer of 2025, only 42 languages in Africa were supported by an LLM, out of more than 2,000 found in the continent; 98 per cent of African languages have no meaningful AI support at all. If you don’t speak one of the languages supported by an LLM, which includes many millions of Africans, then you are denied the opportunities of AI. Just as Africa was excluded from the benefits of past industrial revolutions, so too is it being locked out of the AI revolution.

非洲使用人工智能所面临的困难还体现在语言方面。截至 2025 年夏季，非洲大陆 2000 多种语言中，只有 42 种得到了大型语言模型的支持；也就是说，98% 的非洲语言根本没有得到真正有意义的人工智能支持。如果你不会说大型语言模型所支持的语言之一——而数以千万计的非洲人正属于这种情况——那么你实际上就被排除在人工智能所带来的机会之外。正如非洲曾被排除在历次工业革命的成果之外一样，如今它也正在被挡在人工智能革命的大门之外。

Linguistic and cultural exclusion shape Africa’s relationship with AI. There is nothing ethically neutral or objective about AI. Despite the designers’ pretensions toward universality, AI’s ethical framework reflects Western assumptions and biases. Deeply embedded in the technology itself are the values of its creators. The growth of AI therefore means the imposition of Western values – very much at the expense of Indigenous frameworks. This is ‘algorithmic colonialism’, in the words of the Ethiopian-born cognitive scientist Abeba Birhane: a new kind of colonialism every bit as destructive as the old.

语言和文化上的排斥塑造着非洲与人工智能之间的关系。人工智能在伦理上绝非中立或客观。尽管其设计者声称追求普遍性，但人工智能的伦理框架实际上反映着西方的假设和偏见。创造者的价值观已经深深嵌入技术本身。因此，人工智能的扩张也意味着西方价值观的扩张，而这种扩张往往以牺牲本土伦理框架为代价。用出生于埃塞俄比亚的认知科学家 Abeba Birhane 的话来说，这就是“算法殖民主义”：一种新的殖民主义，其破坏性丝毫不亚于旧式殖民主义。

In order to grasp the seriousness of this new colonialism, we should compare the Western values that undergird AI today with the Hunhu/Ubuntu ethical framework. ‘Hunhu/Ubuntu’ is the name given to the dominant moral tradition of sub-Saharan Africa, expressed in the Nguni/Shona proverb *Umuntu ngumuntu ngabantu* – a person is a person through other persons. Known as Hunhu in Shona-speaking communities of Zimbabwe and Zambia, and as Ubuntu in the Nguni languages of southern Africa, the tradition extends across a vast swathe of the African continent.

为了理解这种新殖民主义有多么严重，我们应该把当今支撑人工智能的西方价值观与 Hunhu/Ubuntu 伦理框架进行比较。“Hunhu/Ubuntu”是撒哈拉以南非洲占主导地位的道德传统的名称，它集中体现在恩古尼语／绍纳语的一句谚语 *Umuntu ngumuntu ngabantu* 中——“一个人通过其他人才成为一个人”。在津巴布韦和赞比亚的绍纳语社群中，这一传统被称为 Hunhu；在南部非洲的恩古尼语族中，则被称为 Ubuntu。它广泛分布于非洲大陆的大片地区。

As Africans increasingly take up the use of AI, there is a real risk that their foundational ethical framework will be diminished or obscured by the subtle imposition through AI of Western values. So we must ask, to what extent are the principles of Hunhu/Ubuntu in conflict with Western values? And how might AI be developed inclusively so that it serves everyone, not just its creators?

随着越来越多的非洲人开始使用人工智能，他们自身基础性的伦理框架面临一个真实的风险：西方价值观可能借助人工智能以潜移默化的方式渗入，从而削弱甚至遮蔽这些伦理传统。因此，我们必须追问：Hunhu/Ubuntu 的原则究竟在多大程度上与西方价值观相冲突？人工智能又应当如何以更加包容的方式发展，使它服务于所有人，而不仅仅服务于它的创造者？

The ideal of ‘responsible AI’ was aptly articulated by the philosopher and computer scientist Damian Okaibedi Eke and colleagues: it is ‘the idea of developing AI systems that will not only be compliant to laws (including human rights provisions) but that are socially/culturally sensitive and acceptable as well as ethically responsible.’ This is a high bar, and AI doesn’t come close to clearing it.

哲学家兼计算机科学家 Damian Okaibedi Eke 及其同事恰当地阐述了“负责任的人工智能”这一理想：它意味着“开发这样的人工智能系统：不仅遵守法律（包括人权条款），而且能够对社会和文化保持敏感、为社会文化所接受，并在伦理上承担责任。”这是一个相当高的标准，而今天的人工智能距离达到这一标准还差得很远。

The major frameworks for ethical AI – guidelines issued by the European Commission’s High-Level Expert Group on AI, by the Organisation for Economic Cooperation and Development, and by UNESCO – share a common vocabulary of fairness, transparency, accountability and human dignity. But the values founding these ideals derive from the Enlightenment liberalism of John Locke, Immanuel Kant and John Stuart Mill. Presented as universal, they are in reality particular, contingent and historically determined. And their particularity has formidable consequences because, when these frameworks are adopted globally, they do not appear as one ethical tradition among many, but as the universal standard – a kind of imperialism dressed up as common sense.

主要的人工智能伦理框架——包括欧盟委员会人工智能高级专家组、经济合作与发展组织（OECD）以及联合国教科文组织（UNESCO）发布的指导原则——都使用着一套共同的词汇：公平、透明、问责和人的尊严。然而，这些理想所依托的价值观源自约翰·洛克、伊曼努尔·康德和约翰·斯图亚特·密尔所代表的启蒙自由主义。它们被呈现为普遍价值，实际上却具有特殊性、偶然性，并由特定历史条件塑造。这种特殊性会产生极其重大的后果，因为当这些框架被推广到全球时，它们并不是以诸多伦理传统中的一种出现，而是被当作普遍标准——一种披着“常识”外衣的帝国主义。

**The baggage of neo-colonialism is found in the essential architecture of AI**

**新殖民主义的包袱，就存在于人工智能最根本的架构之中。**

Part of the problem is that these guidelines have not received enough input from African scholars and thinkers. A 2023 survey found that, of the then-existing 200 sets of AI ethics guidelines identified internationally, the vast majority originated in the Global North: North America, Europe and East Asia. Africa, home to around a billion people, contributed just a handful. This means that the ethical architecture being built into AI systems has been constructed almost entirely without reference to the moral traditions of a continent. The result is a technology that contradicts African values.

问题的一部分在于，这些指导原则并没有充分吸收非洲学者和思想家的意见。2023 年的一项调查发现，在当时全球已确认的约 200 套人工智能伦理准则中，绝大多数来自全球北方——北美、欧洲和东亚。拥有约十亿人口的非洲所贡献的准则却屈指可数。这意味着，被嵌入人工智能系统的伦理架构几乎完全是在没有参考整个非洲大陆道德传统的情况下构建出来的。结果便是一种与非洲价值观相冲突的技术。

So what are the Western values embedded in AI? They can be boiled down to three: rationality, individualism and, most importantly, autonomy. The Western tradition places the individual at the centre of moral life. This is true across its major ethical theories. For Kant, every person is a sovereign lawgiver, an end in themselves. For the utilitarians, what matters is the aggregate of individual satisfactions; happiness is the only metric that matters. Even virtue ethics, the oldest of the three traditions dating back to Plato, locates moral excellence in the cultivation of the individual soul. They all share an assumption that the basic unit of ethics is the person, solitary and unencumbered. AI’s emphasis on individualism over communalism stems from this historical background.

那么，究竟有哪些西方价值观被嵌入人工智能之中？大体可以归结为三项：理性、个人主义，以及最重要的——自主性。西方传统把个人置于道德生活的中心，这一点贯穿其主要伦理理论。对于康德而言，每个人都是拥有主权的立法者，是目的本身。对于功利主义者而言，重要的是个人满足的总和；幸福是唯一真正重要的衡量标准。甚至可以追溯到柏拉图的、三种传统中最古老的德性伦理学，也把道德卓越定位于个人灵魂的培养。这些理论都共享一个假设：伦理的基本单位是孤立且不受牵绊的个人。人工智能之所以强调个人主义而不是共同体主义，正源自这一历史背景。

Autonomy is the most vivid expression of this kind of individualism. As the South African philosopher Mogobe Ramose has argued, the Enlightenment principle of autonomy was bound up with a specific claim about who counted as fully rational – that is, white European men – and therefore who had the standing to govern themselves and others. This idea of autonomy is now coming to the Global South as part of the baggage of neo-colonialism, found in the essential architecture of AI: in the personalisation engines that treat each user as a sovereign individual with preferences to be satisfied, in the decision-making algorithms that optimise for individual outcomes, and in the governance frameworks that emphasise individual rights and individual accountability.

自主性是这种个人主义最鲜明的表现。正如南非哲学家 Mogobe Ramose 所指出的，启蒙时代的自主原则与一种特定主张紧密相连：究竟谁才算是具有完整理性的主体——当时的答案是欧洲白人男性——因而谁才有资格治理自己以及治理他人。如今，这种自主性观念作为新殖民主义包袱的一部分进入全球南方，并存在于人工智能最根本的架构之中：个性化引擎把每个用户视为拥有待满足偏好的独立主权个体；决策算法以优化个人结果为目标；治理框架则强调个人权利和个人责任。

But Hunhu/Ubuntu ethics do not mesh well with these commitments, and the clash between these two value systems runs deeper than cultural emphasis. It goes to the most fundamental and searching question of what a person actually is, in the final analysis.

然而，Hunhu/Ubuntu 伦理与这些价值取向并不能很好地契合，而且这两种价值体系之间的冲突远比文化侧重点的不同更加深刻。归根结底，它触及一个最根本也最深刻的问题：一个“人”究竟是什么？

What is Hunhu/Ubuntu? Much of this framework can best be understood through proverbs, the most important of which is the aforementioned *Umuntu ngubuntu ngabantu*: a person is a person through other persons. The Kenyan philosopher John S Mbiti gave it its classic formulation: ‘I am because we are; since we are, therefore I am.’ This is a statement about the basic structure of human existence that is in serious tension with the Western view of individualised and atomised humans. The Hunhu/Ubuntu claim is that personhood is not something you are born with, but more like something you become, through dealings with other people. As the Nigerian-born philosopher Michael Onyebuchi Eze puts it, ‘a person’s humanity is dependent on the appreciation, preservation and affirmation of other person’s humanity.’

那么，Hunhu/Ubuntu 究竟是什么？理解这一框架的最佳方式之一，是从谚语入手。其中最重要的就是前面提到的 *Umuntu ngubuntu ngabantu*：一个人通过其他人才成为一个人。肯尼亚哲学家 John S Mbiti 对此作出了经典表述：“因为我们存在，所以我存在；既然我们存在，因此我才存在。”这是一个关于人类存在基本结构的命题，它与西方把人理解为个人化、原子化个体的观点存在严重张力。Hunhu/Ubuntu 所主张的是：人格并不是与生俱来的某种东西，而更像是一个人在与他人的交往之中逐渐成为的东西。正如出生于尼日利亚的哲学家 Michael Onyebuchi Eze 所说：“一个人的人性取决于对他人人性的欣赏、维护和肯定。”

Hunhu/Ubuntu is a product of what I have termed the common moral position, which is built on four pillars: the group, dialogue, experience, and spirituality. The group is primary. *Rume rimwe harikombi churu*, as the Shona say: one man cannot surround an anthill. Moral knowledge is not acquired through individual reason working alone, a solitary discovery of what is good and bad, but through ongoing, evolving dialogue, above all at the *dare*, the communal court where elders mediate between the living and the ancestral world. Hunhu/Ubuntu ethics are also anchored in spirituality, meaning that the source of the African value system is the spirit world. Elders discover this ethical knowledge through experience, and they pass it on to the next generations. The Nigerian proverb is apt: what a father sees seated, the son cannot see standing.

Hunhu/Ubuntu 源自我所称的“共同道德立场”，它建立在四根支柱之上：群体、对话、经验和灵性。其中，群体居于首要位置。正如绍纳人的谚语 *Rume rimwe harikombi churu* 所说：“一个人无法围住一座蚁丘。”道德知识并不是靠个人理性独自运作、孤立地发现何为善恶而获得的，而是在持续不断、不断演进的对话中形成的，尤其是在 *dare*——共同体法庭——之中。在那里，长者充当现世生者与祖先世界之间的中介。Hunhu/Ubuntu 伦理同样扎根于灵性，这意味着非洲价值体系的源头来自精神世界。长者通过经验发现这些伦理知识，再将它们传递给下一代。尼日利亚有一句恰如其分的谚语：父亲坐着所看到的东西，儿子即使站着也未必看得到。

The values that flow from these foundations are demanding. Community loyalty: everything you do should benefit the group you belong to; Hunhu/Ubuntu discourages self-seeking tendencies. Consensus-building: no single view carries the day until the collective has spoken. Of great importance when examining the differences between Hunhu/Ubuntu ethics and Western ethics is the issue of human dignity. In one of his most influential contributions, the American-born philosopher Thaddeus Metz argues that a human being has dignity insofar as she has the capacity for community, where community is characterised in terms of solidarity. An African cannot be said to have dignity if he or she does not contribute to the betterment of his or her group or community: ‘I am because we are; since we are, therefore I am.’ Shared responsibility, another important value, means that people and groups come together to find a solution to a common problem affecting their lives. Within the context of Hunhu/Ubuntu, challenges are not left to one individual to solve. *Vanhu* (the people) work together.

由这些基础产生的价值观对人提出了相当高的要求。首先是对共同体的忠诚：你所做的一切都应当有益于自己所属的群体；Hunhu/Ubuntu 不鼓励自利倾向。其次是建立共识：在共同体表达意见之前，任何单一观点都不能独占上风。在考察 Hunhu/Ubuntu 伦理与西方伦理之间的差异时，一个极其重要的问题是人的尊严。出生于美国的哲学家 Thaddeus Metz 在其最具影响力的论述之一中主张，一个人之所以具有尊严，在于其拥有参与共同体的能力，而共同体则以团结互助为特征。如果一个非洲人不能为其群体或共同体的改善作出贡献，那么就不能说他或她真正拥有尊严：“因为我们存在，所以我存在；既然我们存在，因此我才存在。”另一个重要价值是共同责任，即人们和群体共同努力，为影响他们生活的共同问题寻找解决办法。在 Hunhu/Ubuntu 的语境中，困难不会被丢给某一个人独自解决。*Vanhu*（人民）共同协作。

The clash between AI and Hunhu/Ubuntu is built into the logic of how AI systems work. AI is configured for the individual: its recommendation engines, personalisation algorithms and user interfaces treat each person as a sovereign agent, in much the same way that Kant and others regarded the inviolable person. The community, in AI’s architecture, is really an aggregate of individuals or a collection of data points, not its own living and organic entity to which those individuals belong and owe obligations. Hunhu/Ubuntu inverts this. As the Ghanaian theologian John S Pobee put it in *Toward an African Theology* (1979): *Cognatus ergo sum* – I belong, therefore I exist. Where AI begins with the individual and asks what she wants, Hunhu/Ubuntu begins with the community and asks what it needs.

人工智能与 Hunhu/Ubuntu 之间的冲突，本身就已经内嵌在人工智能系统运作的逻辑之中。人工智能是围绕个人设计的：它的推荐引擎、个性化算法和用户界面都把每个人视为拥有主权的自主行动者，这与康德等思想家把个人视为不可侵犯主体的方式十分相似。在人工智能的架构中，“共同体”实际上只是个人的总和或数据点的集合，而不是一个具有自身生命力的有机实体——个人属于这个实体，并对它负有义务。Hunhu/Ubuntu 则恰恰相反。正如加纳神学家 John S Pobee 在 *Toward an African Theology*（《迈向一种非洲神学》，1979）中所说：*Cognatus ergo sum*——“我属于其中，所以我存在。”人工智能从个人出发，问“她想要什么”；Hunhu/Ubuntu 则从共同体出发，问“共同体需要什么”。

This leads to several specific – and illuminating – conflicts between these two systems. The first is over agency. Hunhu/Ubuntu holds that meaningful human agency is collective, that the *dare* (communal court) is not a constraint on individual freedom but its greatest expression. AI substitutes machine agency for human collective agency: it makes decisions, ranks options, filters information and personalises outputs in ways that bypass deliberative community processes entirely.

由此，两种体系之间产生了若干具体而富有启发性的冲突。第一项冲突涉及行动主体性。Hunhu/Ubuntu 认为，有意义的人类主体性是集体性的，*dare*（共同体法庭）并不是对个人自由的限制，反而是个人自由最高程度的体现。而人工智能则以机器的主体性取代人类的集体主体性：它进行决策、排列选项、筛选信息并提供个性化输出，而这些过程完全绕过了共同体内部的协商和 deliberation。

**AI nudges users to look out for themselves; Hunhu/Ubuntu tends toward looking out for each other**

**人工智能推动用户首先为自己着想；Hunhu/Ubuntu 则倾向于让人们彼此照顾。**

A second conflict is about the sacred. Hunhu/Ubuntu situates human life within a cosmological order in which God (*Musikavanhu/Unkulunkulu/Nkosi*) is the ultimate source of creative power, and human beings are stewards within that order rather than masters of it. AI’s ambitions are, meanwhile, unbounded: its most prominent proselytisers have proclaimed that the goal is to build machines that surpass human intelligence across all domains; to replicate the very capacities that the Hunhu/Ubuntu tradition holds to be divine. AI’s aspirations are rank hubris.

第二项冲突涉及神圣性。Hunhu/Ubuntu 把人类生命置于一种宇宙秩序之中，在这一秩序里，上帝（*Musikavanhu/Unkulunkulu/Nkosi*）是创造力量的终极来源，人类只是这一秩序的守护者，而不是它的主人。相比之下，人工智能的野心似乎没有边界：一些最著名的人工智能鼓吹者已经宣称，他们的目标是制造在所有领域都超越人类智慧的机器；换句话说，就是复制那些在 Hunhu/Ubuntu 传统中被视为神圣的能力。从这一角度看，人工智能的抱负就是彻头彻尾的狂妄。

These tensions are visible in the way people actually use AI, and in the ways AI shapes behaviour over time. For instance, AI platforms optimise for individual economic advantage: personalised pricing, competitive job-matching algorithms, financial tools designed to maximise individual returns. Hunhu/Ubuntu’s economic intuition is the opposite. As researchers examining Ubuntu-based approaches to data governance have noted, the philosophy favours sharing over competition and equitable distribution over individual accumulation, what one study termed an ‘anticapitalist logic’ rooted in the principle that wealth has meaning only insofar as it benefits the community. AI meanwhile nudges users to look out for themselves; Hunhu/Ubuntu tends toward looking out for each other.

这些张力既体现在人们实际使用人工智能的方式中，也体现在人工智能随着时间推移塑造人们行为的方式中。例如，人工智能平台往往以个人经济利益最大化为目标：个性化定价、竞争性的职位匹配算法，以及旨在最大化个人收益的金融工具。Hunhu/Ubuntu 的经济直觉恰恰相反。研究基于 Ubuntu 的数据治理方法的学者指出，这一哲学更看重分享而不是竞争，更强调公平分配而不是个人积累。有一项研究将其称为一种“反资本主义逻辑”，其根本原则是：财富只有在造福共同体时才具有意义。与此同时，人工智能会推动用户首先顾及自己；Hunhu/Ubuntu 则倾向于让人们彼此照顾。

Or take decision-making. AI systems increasingly make or pre-structure consequential decisions about creditworthiness, medical triage and job applications on behalf of individuals, cutting out the communal deliberation that Hunhu/Ubuntu regards as morally essential. When an algorithm decides who gets a loan, there is no *dare*.

再以决策为例。人工智能系统越来越多地代表个人作出或预先构造一些具有重大后果的决定，包括信用资格、医疗分诊以及求职申请等，而这恰恰把 Hunhu/Ubuntu 认为在道德上不可或缺的共同体协商过程排除在外。当一个算法决定谁能够获得贷款时，那里并不存在 *dare*。

A third domain is privacy. Western AI ethics frames privacy as an individual right: your data belongs to you. But in many African contexts, information is not individual property but flows through households, clans and communities; decisions about data-sharing are properly made collectively, not by lone individuals. The entire architecture of digital consent assumes a model of personhood that goes against Hunhu/Ubuntu values.

第三个领域是隐私。西方人工智能伦理把隐私定义为一种个人权利：你的数据属于你自己。然而，在许多非洲社会语境中，信息并不是个人财产，而是在家庭、氏族和共同体之间流动；有关数据共享的决定应当由集体作出，而不是由孤立的个人作出。整个数字化“同意”机制的架构，都预设了一种与 Hunhu/Ubuntu 价值观相抵触的人格模式。

The question, then, is: can Hunhu/Ubuntu principles be infused into AI to make it more inclusive? AI developers must prioritise Indigenous peoples and values. For example, the Geneva-based AI expert Jovan Kurbalija has argued that ‘when AI platforms provide advice to African citizens on family issues, health problems, and searching for jobs, to name a few, they have to reflect the local ethical and cultural milieu.’ This is the bare minimum that responsible AI requires. However, the reality on the ground is different. In 2025, *African Business* magazine noted that the vast majority of global training data reflects the experiences and priorities of high-income countries. This imbalance underscores the importance of developing local datasets and content that reflect African contexts.

于是，问题便成为：能否把 Hunhu/Ubuntu 原则融入人工智能，使其更加包容？人工智能开发者必须把本土人民及其价值观置于优先位置。例如，常驻日内瓦的人工智能专家 Jovan Kurbalija 曾主张：“当人工智能平台就家庭问题、健康问题、求职等事项向非洲公民提供建议时，它们必须反映当地的伦理和文化环境。”这只是“负责任的人工智能”最基本的要求。然而，现实情况却并非如此。2025 年，《African Business》杂志指出，全球绝大多数训练数据反映的是高收入国家的经验和优先事项。这种失衡凸显了开发本地数据集和内容的重要性，使人工智能真正能够反映非洲社会的具体语境。

A Hunhu/Ubuntu-informed AI would prioritise collective agency over individual preference, serve the common good rather than aggregate personal satisfactions, and build in transparency and accountability to the communities affected. And it would take environmental sustainability as an expression of Hunhu/Ubuntu’s commitment to the symbiotic relationship between humanity and nature, and to the intergenerational obligations that bind together the past and the future.

一个受到 Hunhu/Ubuntu 启发的人工智能，会把集体主体性置于个人偏好之上，以共同利益而不是个人满足的简单总和为目标，同时在系统中建立面向受影响共同体的透明度和问责机制。它还会把环境可持续性视为 Hunhu/Ubuntu 价值观的一种体现：人类与自然之间存在共生关系，而过去与未来之间则通过跨世代义务彼此连接。

**Translating Hunhu/Ubuntu values into the rules that AI governance requires is exquisitely hard work**

**把 Hunhu/Ubuntu 的价值观转化为人工智能治理所需要的具体规则，是一项极其艰难而精细的工作。**

There are, however, fundamental structural problems to accomplishing this. Africa has been excluded from the benefits of past industrial revolutions, just as it is from the current one, and according to the same logic: the Global North designs and exports the technology, forcing the Global South to accept it without any say in its standards or applications – a merely updated version of the old colonial harm.

然而，要实现这一目标，还存在一些根本性的结构问题。非洲曾经被排除在历次工业革命所带来的利益之外，如今在这场新的工业革命中依然如此，而且背后的逻辑并没有改变：全球北方设计并输出技术，迫使全球南方在无法参与制定技术标准或决定技术应用方式的情况下接受它——这不过是旧式殖民伤害的一个更新版本。

And it’s worth wondering whether AI developers would cede ground in the first place by adopting a Hunhu/Ubuntu ethical framework. What’s in it for them? There is also the question of abstraction: Hunhu/Ubuntu speaks with great power about interconnectedness, solidarity and communal dignity, but translating those values into the specific, enforceable rules that AI governance requires is exquisitely hard work. Finally, the individualism embedded in AI reflects values that urbanisation and modernity have spread widely, including across Africa itself. A Hunhu/Ubuntu framework would need to reckon with that complexity.

此外，我们还值得追问：人工智能开发者最初是否愿意通过采纳 Hunhu/Ubuntu 伦理框架而作出让步？这对他们又有什么好处？还有一个抽象性的问题：Hunhu/Ubuntu 在论述相互联结、团结以及共同体尊严时具有强大的思想力量，但要把这些价值转化为人工智能治理所要求的具体、可执行规则，却是一项极其艰难而精细的工作。最后，嵌入人工智能之中的个人主义，也反映了城市化和现代性已经广泛传播的价值，而这种传播同样发生在非洲内部。一个 Hunhu/Ubuntu 框架必须正视这种复杂性。

What is needed, above all, is a dialogue, a genuine renegotiation of the values that AI exemplifies. Fortunately, the philosophical inspiration for that conversation exists: we have only to turn, once again, to the proverbs – *Rume rimwe harikombi churu* – one person cannot surround an anthill. The task of building an AI that works for all of humanity is a collective one.

最需要的，归根结底，是一场对话——一场对人工智能所体现的价值观进行真正重新协商的对话。幸运的是，这场对话所需要的哲学启示早已存在：我们只需要再次回到那句谚语——*Rume rimwe harikombi churu*——“一个人无法围住一座蚁丘。”构建一种能够为全人类服务的人工智能，本身就是一项必须共同完成的事业。
